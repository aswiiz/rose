Dear Pavithra,

Do you remember when we were classmates in Class 10? You were such a good friend to me, and I’m genuinely happy that our story began with friendship.

Somewhere around May, I started noticing that my feelings for you were becoming something more. Since then, they’ve only grown stronger. Your birthday on May 11 is a special day, but what has become even more special to me is getting to know you, spending time with you, and seeing how happy we can be together.

I love your cute smile, your beautiful eyes, and your voice. I love the way you take charge and handle things. And I’m so grateful for all the ways you’ve helped me. You’ve been there for me, and that means more than I can explain.

These past months, our time together has made me feel so happy. I don’t want to rush or take anything for granted. I just want you to know that my feelings are real, and I would love to build something beautiful with you—one day, one memory, and one little moment at a time.

I dream of a future where we keep supporting each other, laughing together, and choosing each other through life. I hope our love keeps growing, for a long, long time.

Pavithra, you started as my good friend, and now you’ve become someone I love deeply.

Would you like to start this relationship with me?

Whatever you feel, I want you to be honest. Your happiness and comfort matter to me.

With all my love,
Aswin Raj ♡
